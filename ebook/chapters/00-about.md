# About this book {.unnumbered}

*Build It Right* teaches the engineering habits behind pstack to people who build software with AI agents and don't have a programming background.

pstack is a collection of skills for AI coding agents by Lauren Tan ("poteto"). It's free and open source at `github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack`. This book is based on pstack version 0.15.5. The principles, playbooks, and quoted passages come from pstack. The explanations, examples, and comparisons are this book's own. Any mistakes in translating her ideas into plain language are mine, not hers.

pstack is copyright 2026 Lauren Tan and is released under the MIT License. The full license text is in the pstack repository.

The product used as an example throughout, ClinicDesk, is made up.
